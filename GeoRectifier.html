How to Georectify

  1. Click 🗺️ Load GeoTIFF Background to display your satellite/aerial image.
  2. Load your Historical DXF (left) and True Field DXF (right) vectors.
  3. Click a landmark on the Historical Map to place a Source Anchor.
  4. Click the matching point on the True Survey Map to complete the pair.
  5. Add 3+ pairs to activate real-time rubber-sheeting!
  6. Click Export Rectified DXF when satisfied!
